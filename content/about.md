## The Studio
Sleepy Puppy Studios is small independent game studio comprised of 5 devs. The studio formed for a capstone project for the University of Washington's Game Design certificate program in Fall 2026, where we began work on our first title: *A Feast Most Deadly*.

![Logo](images/SleepyPuppyStudiosLogo.png)

### Meet the Team
**Anna Roth** is our Lead Narrative Designer and Lead UI/UX Designer. She writes all the in-game text for *A Feast Most Deadly* as well as spearheading the worldbuilding and narrative for the game. But Anna does not just write beautiful words, she also leads the design of UI/UX, which is absolutely crucial for the success of our game.

**Dante MacLennan** is our Lead Game Designer. He helped create and refine the mechanical systems that drive the fun of the game -- from how the menu planning should work, to the structure of the gameplay loop, and to how players should be scored.

**Eli Cantrell** is our Producer and Audio Engineer. In addition to keeping the team on task and on scope, he also is in charge for all the audio of the game -- tasked with sourcing and editing sound effects and even creating the original soundtrack for the game (something he has never done before!).

**Kevin Arne** is our Lead Programmer. Using the Godot Engine, Kevin is responsible for making sure the game is actually playable by programming the systems and gameplay that envisioned on paper.

**Lily Ngo** is our Art Director and Senior Programmer. Not only did she help define the artistic direction of our game, but also made all of the artwork herself! When she is not drawing regal characters or delicious dishes, Lily also provides her expertise in programming.

Sleepy Puppy Studios is truly a team effort and every member of our team provides support and effort beyond the titles you see listed above.
