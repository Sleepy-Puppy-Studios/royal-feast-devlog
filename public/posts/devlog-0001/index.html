Prepping the Meal

We here at Sleepy Puppy Studios have been hard at work on our first project, a deductive reasoning game set in a fictional kingdom at the outset of the modern age.

In A Feast Most Deadly you play as the head steward for a finicky royal family with very particular tastes. You are in charge of assembling their dinner menu each night and making sure that they– and their guests– are satisfied.

Over the course of three in-game days, you must discover and deduce information about each attendee of that night’s feast to uncover what the best possible menu is. Alternatively, you may even to decide to favor some attendees over others, but you risk losing affinity with those attendees, which could have political ramifications for the king. Talk to guests and gather rumors to determine what people want and what they don’t want. Do you want to create a menu that everyone merely LIKES or a menu that some people LOVE and others HATE?
